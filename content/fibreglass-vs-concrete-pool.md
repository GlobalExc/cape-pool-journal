faqs:
- q: Which lasts longer, fibreglass or concrete?
  a: Both can last decades when well built and maintained. A concrete structure is very long-lived, but its interior finish needs resurfacing over time. A fibreglass shell's gelcoat can fade or blister with age and poor water balance.
- q: Do fibreglass pools use fewer chemicals?
  a: Often, yes. The smooth, non-porous surface gives algae less to cling to, and it doesn't affect the pH the way fresh plaster or marbelite can.
- q: Can a fibreglass pool pop out of the ground?
  a: An empty fibreglass pool can lift if groundwater is high. That's one reason not to drain any pool without professional advice, especially in winter.
- q: Which is better for a sloping or oddly shaped garden?
  a: Concrete, because it can be designed to fit the space. Fibreglass needs enough access for a crane or truck and a site that suits the shell's shape.

## Side-by-side

| | Fibreglass | Concrete (gunite or shotcrete) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape and size | Set range of moulds | Anything you can design |
| Installation time | Fast, often days once excavated | Several weeks |
| Upfront cost | Usually lower for standard sizes | Usually higher |
| Surface feel | Smooth gelcoat | Marbelite, pebble or tiles (can feel rougher) |
| Maintenance | Low, and less algae grip | Brushing matters more. Resurface every so often. |
| Chemical use | Generally lower | Fresh finishes affect the pH at first |
| Site access | Needs crane or truck access | Can be built with tighter access |
| Repairs | Gelcoat repairs are specialised | Patching and resurfacing are common |

## Choose fibreglass if…

- A standard size and shape fits your garden
- You want the pool quickly
- Low maintenance matters more than custom design

## Choose concrete if…

- You want a custom shape, an infinity edge or a special depth
- The site is sloped or tight
- You're building a long-term "forever home" feature

## Lifespan and upkeep

Whatever you choose, **water balance protects the surface**. Low pH and soft water etch marbelite and age gelcoat. High calcium leaves scale. Keep to the ranges in our [pool chemical levels chart](/pool-chemical-levels-chart/).

## In Cape Town: groundwater and access

Winter rain raises the groundwater in many suburbs. That affects excavation and makes draining any pool risky. Narrow side access, common on older properties, can rule out a fibreglass shell. Have a builder check both on site before you decide.
