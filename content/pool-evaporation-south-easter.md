- q: How much water does a pool lose to evaporation?
  a: It varies with wind, sun, temperature and humidity. On hot, windy Cape Town weeks, a visible drop of several centimetres is common. If the loss seems unusually high, do the bucket test.
- q: Does a pool cover really stop evaporation?
  a: A well-fitting cover prevents most evaporation while it's on, and also keeps out sand and leaves. The more hours it's on, the more water you save.
- q: Can I top up my pool with borehole water?
  a: Often yes, but test it first. Borehole water can contain iron or manganese that stains the pool. A metal sequestrant helps.

## Why Cape Town pools lose so much water

Evaporation speeds up when:

- **Wind** blows across the surface. The south-easter does exactly this from about November to March.
- **The air is dry** and the sun is strong.
- **The water is warm**, especially in heated pools.
- **Water features** spray water into the air.

## Step 1: Rule out a leak with the bucket test

1. Fill a bucket with pool water and put it on the first or second pool step, so it sits in the pool water.
2. Mark the water level **inside** the bucket and **on the outside** (the pool level).
3. Switch off any auto-fill, and run the pump as normal.
4. After 24 hours, compare the two drops.

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pool and bucket drop by about the same amount | Evaporation |
| The pool drops noticeably more than the bucket | Likely a leak. Call a professional. |

## Step 2: Cut evaporation

| Measure | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Pool cover** (solar, slatted or safety) | The biggest saving. It also keeps out sand and leaves. |
| **Windbreaks** (hedges, screens, fencing) | Slows wind across the surface |
| **Turn off water features on windy days** | Spraying water evaporates fast |
| **Lower the heating temperature** | Warm water evaporates faster |
| **Keep the water level at mid-skimmer** | Avoids losing water to splash-out |

## Step 3: Top up responsibly

- Check the **City of Cape Town's current water rules** before topping up from the mains.
- Top up in the evening, when less evaporates.
- After a big top-up, retest the pH, alkalinity, calcium and chlorine. Fresh water changes all four.

## Wind brings sand too

The same south-easter fills pools with sand and dust. Empty the baskets more often in windy weeks, and vacuum once the wind drops. See our guide on [how to vacuum a pool properly](/how-to-vacuum-a-pool/).
