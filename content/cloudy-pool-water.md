- q: How long does cloudy pool water take to clear?
  a: With balanced water and a working filter, most cloudy pools clear in 24–72 hours. A clarifier speeds this up. Floc settles dirt overnight, so you can vacuum it out the next morning.
- q: Is cloudy pool water safe to swim in?
  a: It's best not to. If you can't clearly see the main drain at the deep end, swimmers in trouble can't be seen either. Cloudiness can also mean the sanitiser isn't working.
- q: What's the difference between clarifier and flocculant?
  a: Clarifier clumps tiny particles together so the filter can catch them, and you keep swimming normally. Flocculant drops the particles to the floor, and you vacuum them to waste the next day. Floc works faster on very cloudy water but loses more water.
- q: Why does my pool go cloudy after shocking?
  a: That's usually dead algae and organic matter. Keep the pump running and backwash. It should clear within a day or two.

## First, find the cause

Cloudiness means tiny particles are suspended in the water. They come from one of three places:

1. **The filter isn't removing them.** The pump runs too few hours, the filter is dirty, or the sand is old.
2. **The water chemistry is creating them.** High pH, high alkalinity or high calcium make minerals come out of solution.
3. **Something is feeding them in.** Dust, pollen, sunscreen, dead algae or early algae growth.

## Step 1: Test the water

| Test | Target | If it's off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free chlorine | 1–3 ppm | Low chlorine lets bacteria and algae cloud the water. Shock. |
| pH | 7.2–7.6 | Above 7.8 causes cloudiness and weak chlorine. Add acid. |
| Total alkalinity | 80–120 ppm | High alkalinity keeps pushing the pH up. Lower it slowly with acid. |
| Calcium hardness | 200–400 ppm | Very high calcium causes scale and haze. |

## Step 2: Fix the filtration

- Empty the skimmer and pump baskets.
- Backwash until the sight glass runs clear, then rinse.
- Run the pump for 24 hours until the water clears.
- If the sand is more than about five years old, or it feels clumpy, it may need replacing.

## Step 3: Shock if the chlorine was low

Dose in the evening so the sun doesn't burn it off. Expect the water to look worse for a few hours before it gets better.

## Step 4: Clarifier or floc

If the water is balanced and the filter is clean but it's still hazy after a day:

- **Light haze:** add pool clarifier as the label directs, and keep filtering.
- **Milky water:** use flocculant. Turn the pump off overnight, then vacuum the settled layer **to waste** slowly the next morning.

## Prevention

- Keep the pH and chlorine in range, and test 2–3 times a week in summer.
- Run the pump long enough. See our guide on [how often a pool should be serviced](/how-often-pool-service-cape-town/).
- Ask swimmers to rinse off sunscreen before getting in.
- Backwash weekly in summer.

## In Cape Town: wind and dust

Cloudy water often follows a south-easter, when fine dust blows in faster than the filter can handle. Run the pump longer for a day after strong wind and backwash once it settles. If cloudiness comes back every time the wind blows, a pool cover saves a lot of work.
