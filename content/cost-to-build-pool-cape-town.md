- q: Is a fibreglass pool cheaper than a concrete pool?
  a: For a standard size and shape, fibreglass usually costs less and installs faster. Concrete costs more but can be built to any shape or size. Our fibreglass vs concrete guide compares them in detail.
- q: How long does it take to build a pool?
  a: A fibreglass pool can be installed in days once the hole is dug and the shell delivered. A concrete pool usually takes several weeks, depending on weather, inspections and finishes.
- q: Do I need plans approved to build a pool in Cape Town?
  a: In most cases, yes. A pool is building work and usually needs approved plans and a compliant safety barrier. Check with the City of Cape Town or ask your pool builder to handle the submission.
- q: What ongoing costs should I budget for?
  a: Electricity for the pump, chemicals, cleaner parts, filter sand, and a service if you use one. Over the years, budget for resurfacing and replacing the pump.

## The cost drivers

| Factor | What pushes the price up |
| --- | --- |
| Pool type | Custom concrete shapes, infinity edges, raised walls |
| Size and depth | More excavation, materials and water |
| Site access | No access for machinery, so digging and removal by hand |
| Ground | Rock, a high water table or sloping ground needing retaining walls |
| Interior finish | Premium marbelite, pebble or full tiling |
| Equipment | Larger or variable-speed pumps, salt chlorinators, automation |
| Surrounds | Paving or decking area, coping material, drainage |
| Extras | Heat pumps, solar heating, lighting, water features, covers |
| Compliance | Plans, engineer's sign-off, safety fencing |

## Budget for the whole project

Homeowners often budget for the pool shell and forget the rest. A complete quote should include:

1. Excavation and removing the soil
2. The shell (fibreglass or concrete) and the interior finish
3. Plumbing, pump, filter and electrical connection
4. Coping and paving
5. A compliant safety barrier
6. The first fill and chemical start-up
7. Rubble removal and repairs to the garden

## How to compare quotes

- Get **at least three itemised quotes** for the same size and spec.
- Check what's **excluded**. Paving, electrics and fencing are common exclusions.
- Ask about **warranties** on the shell, finish and equipment.
- Ask for **references** and photos of recent local builds.
- Confirm **who handles plan approval**.

## Ways to save

- Choose a standard fibreglass shape if it suits your garden.
- Keep the depth moderate. Very deep pools cost more to build, heat and maintain.
- Spend on a good pump and filter. They cost less to run over the life of the pool.
- Add extras like heating or automation later. Ask for the plumbing to be prepared for them now.

## In Cape Town: site conditions matter

Sandy Cape Flats soil, rocky slopes on the mountain side and high groundwater in winter all change how a pool is built and what it costs. A site visit before quoting is essential. Be wary of any builder who quotes without one.
