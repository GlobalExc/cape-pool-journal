faqs:
- q: How many people can safely swim in my pool at once?
  a: It depends on the size, depth and how well people can swim. Fewer swimmers is safer and keeps the water cleaner. Have weaker swimmers and children in the shallow end, and watch the water constantly.
- q: Should I shock the pool before or after a party?
  a: Both. Shock the evening before so the chlorine is in range by the next day, and again the evening after to clear the sweat, sunscreen and germs swimmers leave behind.
- q: Can I use glasses and bottles near the pool?
  a: Avoid it. Broken glass in or near a pool is dangerous and very hard to find. Some pools need draining to find it. Use plastic or metal cups.
- q: How do I keep the pool water clear during a big party?
  a: Run the pump all day, empty the skimmer basket halfway through, and check the chlorine. Ask guests to rinse off before swimming.

## Two days before

- [ ] Test the pH, chlorine and alkalinity. Correct them now, not on the day.
- [ ] Brush and vacuum the pool.
- [ ] Backwash and rinse the filter.
- [ ] Check the gate latch, the fence and the life ring or reaching pole.
- [ ] Check the pool lights if you'll swim after dark.

## The evening before

- [ ] Shock the pool.
- [ ] Run the pump overnight.
- [ ] Put out shade (an umbrella or gazebo) and plenty of towels.

## On the day: safety

- [ ] Name a **water watcher** who stays sober and off their phone, and swap every 15–20 minutes.
- [ ] Keep a reaching pole and a life ring by the pool.
- [ ] Have weak swimmers in the shallow end, with armbands or life jackets for children.
- [ ] Set a no running and no diving rule in the shallow end.
- [ ] Keep the gate closed when nobody is swimming.
- [ ] Have sunscreen, water and a first-aid kit within reach.

## On the day: food and drink

- **No glass at the pool.** Use plastic or metal cups.
- **Braai and food tables away from the edge.** Crumbs and oil end up in the skimmer.
- **Plenty of water.** Swimmers in the sun dehydrate fast.

## During the party

- [ ] Run the pump all day.
- [ ] Empty the skimmer basket at lunchtime.
- [ ] Top up the chlorine mid-afternoon on a hot, busy day.

## The day after

- [ ] Skim and empty the baskets.
- [ ] Shock the pool in the evening.
- [ ] Brush the waterline, where sunscreen leaves a greasy ring.
- [ ] Backwash the next morning.
- [ ] Retest and rebalance.
